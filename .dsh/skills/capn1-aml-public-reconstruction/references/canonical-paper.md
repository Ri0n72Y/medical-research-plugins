# Canonical paper

## Identity

Houcai Wang et al.

**CAPN1 is a novel biomaker of patients with AML based on comprehensive analysis**

Public index: PubMed PMID 37114994.

The indexed title contains the spelling "biomaker"; retain the official indexed wording when citing it.

## Publicly recoverable method shape

Public metadata and abstract-level material establish a workflow involving:

- AML public data from TCGA;
- R-based analysis;
- CAPN1 expression / clinical association;
- survival / prognosis analysis;
- differential features;
- GO / KEGG functional analysis;
- DAVID;
- STRING;
- CIBERSORT;
- ssGSEA;
- immune-infiltration analysis.

This resource is a method anchor, not a claim that every original parameter is publicly known.

## v0 interpretation

The project reconstructs the study from current public data and public tooling.

It does not claim to reproduce:

- the authors' exact downloaded TCGA snapshot;
- unpublished preprocessing code;
- unavailable package versions;
- unavailable cutoffs or thresholds;
- licensed CIBERSORT assets.

Unknown material parameters become explicit researcher decisions or declared public substitutes.

## Scientific boundary

The paper's public-data associations are computational evidence.

Do not turn an expression, survival, enrichment, or immune association into a causal CAPN1 mechanism without independent experimental evidence.
